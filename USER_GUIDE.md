# Wichita Life — Admin Guide

A plain-English guide to running the app day to day. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Getting in
Open your app URL and sign in with your email + password. You land on the
**Dashboard** — your "what needs attention today" home screen.

## Adding a sponsor
1. **Sponsors → New sponsor**.
2. Fill in the company name (the only required field) and whatever else you have:
   contacts, contract dates, price, billing frequency, payment method.
3. Under **Package assignment**, either pick a package, choose **Custom
   (à la carte)**, or leave it as **No package** for now.
4. **Create sponsor.**

*Have a spreadsheet of sponsors?* **Sponsors → Import** to bring them in at once
(download the template first). Duplicates are skipped automatically.

## Creating a package
1. **Packages → New package**.
2. Name it, set a base price and billing frequency.
3. Add the deliverables it includes (e.g. *Email — Headline ×2*, *Social post ×1*).
4. **Create package.** Use the **⋯** menu on a package to Edit, Duplicate, or
   Deactivate it.

## Assigning a package (and going à la carte)
- On a sponsor, set the **Package** field to a package (or **Custom (à la carte)**).
- Click **Manage deliverables** on the sponsor to fine-tune: change quantities,
  add or remove deliverables, set a **custom price** (same package, different price
  is fine), and toggle auto-generation. Anything custom flags the sponsor as
  **Customized**.

## Generating monthly deliverables
1. **Deliverables** → pick the month with the arrows.
2. **Generate monthly deliverables** → review the **preview** (what's new vs. what
   already exists) → confirm.
3. It's safe to run again anytime — it never creates duplicates.

## Scheduling sponsor content (calendar)
1. **Calendar** → pick the month.
2. **Auto-schedule emails** creates a newsletter slot on your Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri
   send days (delete any single day for a holiday).
3. Click a day's **+** to add a one-off slot (Instagram post, dedicated email, etc.).
4. Click a slot → **Assign** an unscheduled deliverable to it. The sidebar shows
   each sponsor's progress ("2/2 scheduled" or "1 still to schedule").
5. Need an extra ad? **Add deliverable** right from the calendar.

## Recording an invoice / payment
This app tracks value and paid status (your real invoices live in Stripe).
1. **Billing** → open a sponsor.
2. You'll see one row per period since their contract start (monthly, quarterly,
   or annual — matching their billing frequency).
3. Adjust the **amount** if needed and click **Mark paid** when they pay.
4. The sponsor's **Overview** shows "Payments up to date" or "N unpaid" at a glance.

## Uploading assets
1. On a sponsor → **Assets** tab → **Upload asset**.
2. Upload a file (logo, photo, contract, ad copy…) or paste an **external link**.
3. Files are private; images show a preview. Delete anytime (with confirmation).

## Carrying forward a deliverable
If something didn't run this month, open it (or use the Deliverables list) and
**Carry forward** — it moves to next month but remembers the month it was
originally owed for.

## Distribution (wholesale & consignment)
1. **Distribution → New drop-off**: pick a location, date, and deal type.
2. Add products (quantity, wholesale price, retail price).
3. **Wholesale** shows what you're owed up front → **Mark paid**.
4. **Consignment**: come back later, **record what sold**, and it computes your
   cut (default 70%) and what's still on the shelf → **Mark settled**.
5. Manage your **Products** and **Locations** in the other tabs.

## Viewing monthly reports
The **Dashboard** shows monthly fulfillment (owed / published / scheduled / % done)
and revenue. Use the month selector to look back or ahead.
